From farm
to final shipment

A structured process from sourcing and production to quality checks and shipment. The steps vary by product, but the focus stays on consistent quality and careful handling at every stage.

Ask about our process
  1. Step 1Sourcing from local farmersCoconuts and husks are bought directly from local growers and checked on arrival.
  2. Step 2Separating and sievingThe husk is separated into fibre, pith and chips, then sieved to the required size.
  3. Step 3WashingThe pith is washed with clean water to lower its salt content, then checked for EC and pH.
  4. Step 4DryingDried evenly to the required moisture level, with moisture checked before the next stage.
  5. Step 5Compressing and packingPressed into blocks of the required size and weight, then packed and palletised.
  6. Step 6Quality checkSamples are checked for EC, pH, moisture and block weight, with records kept for traceability.
  7. Step 7ShippingPallets are loaded and secured in containers, with updates on dispatch and delivery.

From block to growing medium

A coco peat block only needs water and a little time to become ready-to-use growing medium.

  1. 1.Use a large containerPlace the block in a clean bucket, tub or tray large enough for it to swell.
  2. 2.Add clean waterPour in clean water slowly.
  3. 3.Let it soakWait 15 to 20 minutes while the block absorbs the water and softens.
  4. 4.Break it upLoosen the block with your hands or a fork until it is light and fluffy.
  5. 5.Rinse if neededIf the peat is unwashed, or you grow salt-sensitive crops, rinse it and check EC before use.
  6. 6.Fill and plantFill pots, bags or trays and plant.
