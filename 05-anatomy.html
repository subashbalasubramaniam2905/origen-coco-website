From Farm to Final Shipment

A structured process from sourcing and production to quality checks and shipment. The steps vary by product, but the focus remains on consistent quality and careful handling at every stage.

1. Coco peat blocks and husk chips
  1. Step 1Sourcing from local farmersWe buy coconuts and husks directly from local growers.
  2. Step 2Separating and sievingThe husk is separated into fibre, pith, and chips.
  3. Step 3WashingThe pith is washed with clean water to reduce its salt content.
  4. Step 4DryingThe washed material is dried to the required moisture level and turned during the process so it dries evenly.
  5. Step 5Compressing and packingThe dried peat is pressed into blocks according to the required size and weight.
  6. Step 6Quality checkSamples are checked against the product specifications, including EC, pH, moisture, and block weight.
  7. Step 7ShippingThe finished pallets are loaded into containers and secured for transport.

How to expand a coco peat block?

  1. 1Place the block in a clean bucket, tub, or tray large enough for it to swell.
  2. 2Pour in clean water slowly.
  3. 3Wait 15 to 20 minutes while the block absorbs the water and softens.
  4. 4Break up the block with your hands or a fork until it is loose and fluffy.
  5. 5If the peat is unwashed, or you grow salt-sensitive crops, rinse it and check EC before use.
  6. 6Fill pots, bags or trays and plant.
