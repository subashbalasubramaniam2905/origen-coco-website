From Farm to Final Shipment

A structured process from sourcing and production to quality checks and shipment. The steps vary by product, but the focus remains on consistent quality and careful handling at every stage.

1. Coco peat blocks and husk chips

  1. 1

    Sourcing from local farmers

    We buy coconuts and husks directly from local growers. Each lot is checked for quality when it arrives, and anything that does not meet our standards is set aside.

  2. 2

    Separating and sieving

    The husk is separated into fibre, pith, and chips. The pith and chips are then sieved to the required size, while oversized pieces and unwanted material are removed.

  3. 3

    Washing

    The pith is washed with clean water to reduce its salt content. We check the EC and pH before it moves on to make sure it is suitable for growing applications.

  4. 4

    Drying

    The washed material is dried to the required moisture level and turned during the process so it dries evenly. Moisture is checked before the material moves to the next stage.

  5. 5

    Compressing and packing

    The dried peat is pressed into blocks according to the required size and weight. The blocks are then packed, stacked, and secured on pallets for storage and transport.

  6. 6

    Quality check

    Samples are checked against the product specifications, including EC, pH, moisture, and block weight. Only batches that meet the requirements are cleared for dispatch, with records kept for traceability.

  7. 7

    Shipping

    The finished pallets are loaded into containers and secured for transport. Shipments travel by road and sea, and customers are kept informed about dispatch and delivery.

How to expand a coco peat block?

  1. 1Place the block in a clean bucket, tub, or tray large enough for it to swell.
  2. 2Pour in clean water slowly.
  3. 3Wait 15 to 20 minutes while the block absorbs the water and softens.
  4. 4Break up the block with your hands or a fork until it is loose and fluffy.
  5. 5If the peat is unwashed, or you grow salt-sensitive crops, rinse it and check EC before use.
  6. 6Fill pots, bags or trays and plant.
